# Interested Parties Register

**Document ID:** AIMS-STAKEHOLDERS-2026-001  
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026  
**Clause:** 4.2 — Understanding the Needs and Expectations of Interested Parties

---

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies all interested parties relevant to NovaTech's AI Management System and their expectations, needs, and requirements.

---

## 2. Interested Parties Register

| Stakeholder Group | Needs & Expectations | Relevance to AIMS | Engagement Method |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Customers** | Fair treatment, transparency, privacy, accuracy | Core — directly impacted by AI decisions | Consumer disclosures, transparency portal |
| **Employees** | Fair hiring, safe work environment, upskilling | High — impacted by AI in HR | Employee handbook, training programs |
| **Regulators** | Compliance, documentation, audit access | Critical — non-compliance triggers fines | Regulatory filings, audit support |
| **Investors** | Risk management, responsible AI, ESG performance | High — AI risk affects valuation | Investor briefings, annual reports |
| **Board of Directors** | Oversight, accountability, risk appetite | Critical — ultimate accountability | Board briefings, quarterly reports |
| **Auditors** | Access, evidence, transparency | High — assurance of compliance | Audit evidence repository |
| **Vendors** | Clear requirements, fair partnership | Medium — AI vendors must comply | Vendor assessments, contracts |
| **Society** | Trustworthy AI, societal benefit | Medium — reputation and license to operate | Public disclosures, transparency portal |
| **Employees (AI Developers)** | Clear guidelines, resources, support | High — need policies and tools | Training, AI CoE support |
