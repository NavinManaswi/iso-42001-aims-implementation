## 1. Policy Statement

NovaTech Financial Group is committed to the responsible development, deployment, and operation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems. We recognize that AI presents significant opportunities to serve our customers and grow our business, but also carries risks that must be managed.

This policy establishes the principles, requirements, and governance structure for AI across our organization. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties that develop, deploy, or operate AI systems on behalf of NovaTech.

---

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Lawfulness
We comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and standards, including but not limited to:
- EU AI Act
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0
- ISO/IEC 42001:2026
- GDPR / CCPA
- ECOA, FHA, and other US fair lending laws

### 2.2 Beneficial Purpose
We design and deploy AI systems that benefit our customers, employees, and society. We do not deploy AI systems that cause harm or violate fundamental rights.

### 2.3 Human Oversight
We maintain meaningful human oversight over high-risk AI systems. We ensure that humans can review, challenge, and override AI decisions where appropriate.

### 2.4 Transparency
We are transparent about our use of AI. We provide clear explanations of AI decisions to affected individuals. We disclose when customers are interacting with an AI system.

### 2.5 Fairness
We proactively identify and mitigate algorithmic bias. We test AI systems for disparate impact across protected characteristics. We monitor fairness continuously.

### 2.6 Accountability
We assign clear ownership and accountability for every AI system. We maintain auditable documentation of AI governance activities.

### 2.7 Continual Improvement
We continuously monitor, evaluate, and improve our AI systems and governance practices. We learn from incidents and adapt to regulatory changes.

### 2.8 Security & Privacy
We protect AI systems from adversarial attacks and unauthorized access. We ensure that personal data is processed in compliance with privacy laws.

## 3. Policy Requirements

| Requirement | Description | Owner |
|-------------|-------------|-------|
| **AI Inventory** | Maintain a complete inventory of all AI systems | CAIO |
| **Risk Classification** | Classify all AI systems using UCCF | AI Governance Council |
| **AI Risk Assessment** | Conduct AIRA for all high-risk systems | Model Risk |
| **Human Oversight** | Implement human-in-the-loop for high-risk systems | System Owners |
| **Bias Audits** | Conduct annual independent bias audits | Compliance |
| **Incident Response** | Maintain AI incident response capability | CISO |
| **Transparency** | Disclose AI use to customers | Product |
| **Vendor Management** | Assess and monitor AI vendors | Procurement |
| **Training** | Provide AI literacy training to all employees | L&D |

## 4. Governance Structure

| Tier | Body | Responsibilities |
|------|------|------------------|

| **Tier 3** | AI Risk Working Group | Operational risk management, control implementation |
