# 🏛️ Leadership Commitment (Clause 5 — Leadership)

## Overview

This folder contains artifacts for **Clause 5** of ISO/IEC 42001 — Leadership. These documents demonstrate executive commitment to the AI Management System.

| File | Clause | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|
| `ai-policy.md` | 5.2 | Board-approved AI Policy establishing the governance framework |
| `roles-responsibilities.md` | 5.3 | RACI matrix and accountability framework |

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Leadership Commitment
Top management must:
- Establish and communicate the AI Policy
- Ensure the AIMS achieves intended outcomes
- Allocate necessary resources
- Promote a culture of responsible AI
- Ensure accountability for AI systems

### AI Policy
The AI Policy must:
- Be appropriate to the organization's purpose
- Include a commitment to satisfy applicable requirements
- Include a commitment to continual improvement
- Be communicated and understood
