## 📖 What Is ISO/IEC 42001?

**ISO/IEC 42001:2026** is the international standard for **AI Management Systems**. It provides a framework for organizations to:

| Objective | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Govern AI Responsibly** | Establish policies, roles, and accountability for AI |
| **Manage AI Risks** | Identify, assess, and mitigate AI-specific risks |
| **Ensure Compliance** | Meet regulatory requirements (EU AI Act, GDPR, etc.) |
| **Build Trust** | Demonstrate responsible AI to customers, regulators, and stakeholders |
| **Continually Improve** | Monitor, measure, and enhance AI governance over time |

**Why It Matters:** ISO 42001 certification is becoming a **market differentiator** — it signals to customers, investors, and regulators that your organization takes AI governance seriously.

## 🗺️ Certification Roadmap

| Phase | Weeks | Key Activities | Deliverables |
|-------|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **Phase 1: Planning & Scoping** | 1-4 | Define AIMS scope, map stakeholders, identify legal obligations | Scope Statement, Stakeholder Register |
| **Phase 2: Leadership & Governance** | 5-8 | Secure Board commitment, establish AI Policy, define roles | AI Policy, RACI Matrix |
| **Phase 3: Risk Management** | 9-14 | Conduct risk & impact assessments, develop SoA | Risk Register, SoA |
| **Phase 4: Implementation** | 15-24 | Implement lifecycle controls, data governance, vendor management | Process Documentation, Monitoring System |
| **Phase 5: Performance Evaluation** | 25-28 | Internal audit, management review, address nonconformities | Audit Report, CAPA Records |
| **Phase 6: Certification** | 29-32 | Gap assessment, Stage 1 & Stage 2 audits | Certificate |
