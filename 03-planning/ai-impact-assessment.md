# AI Impact Assessment

**Document ID:** AIMS-IA-2026-001  
**Date:** 15 August 2026  
**Clause:** 6.1.3 — AI Impact Assessment  
**System:** CreditIQ

---

## 1. Purpose

This document assesses the potential impacts of the CreditIQ AI system on individuals, society, and fundamental rights, in accordance with ISO/IEC 42001:2026, Clause 6.1.3.

---

## 2. Impact Assessment

### 2.1 Human Rights Impact

| Human Right | Potential Impact | Severity | Mitigation |
|-------------|------------------|----------|------------|
| **Non-Discrimination** | Biased credit decisions based on race/gender | Critical | Bias mitigation; quarterly audits |
| **Privacy** | Unauthorized access to personal data | High | Differential privacy; access controls |
| **Access to Justice** | Denied credit without adequate explanation | High | LIME explanations; human review |
| **Economic Well-being** | Denied credit during economic hardship | High | Economic drift monitoring |
| **Due Process** | Lack of meaningful human oversight | Medium | Human-in-the-loop controls |

### 2.2 Societal Impact

| Impact | Description | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|-------------|----------|------------|
| **Access to Credit** | Denied credit to underserved communities | Critical | Bias mitigation; fair lending controls |
| **Economic Inclusion** | Over-reliance on automated scoring | Medium | Human review; appeal process |
| **Market Competition** | Advantage to those with better data | Low | Competitive monitoring |

### 2.3 Organizational Impact

| Impact | Description | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|-------------|----------|------------|
| **Reputation** | Bias allegations damage trust | High | Bias audits; transparency |
| **Regulatory** | Fines and enforcement actions | High | Compliance controls |
| **Financial** | $440M+ settlement exposure | Critical | Proactive governance |
