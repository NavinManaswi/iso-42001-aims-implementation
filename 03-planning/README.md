## 🔑 Key Concepts

### AI Risk Assessment
- Identify risks associated with AI systems
- Analyze likelihood and impact
- Evaluate risks and determine treatment

### AI Impact Assessment
- Assess the societal and human rights impacts of AI systems
- Address fundamental rights risks

### Statement of Applicability (SoA)
- Maps Annex A controls to the organization
- Justifies inclusion or exclusion of each control
